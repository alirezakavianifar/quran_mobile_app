## ⚡ Quick Steps to Upload on Upwork

1. Log in to your Upwork profile and navigate to **"Portfolio"** -> **"Add a new portfolio project"**.
2. Open [`UPWORK_PROJECT_DETAILS.md`](./UPWORK_PROJECT_DETAILS.md) and copy:
   - **Project title**: `Quran AI Platform & Mobile App (.NET 8, Flutter, RAG AI, pgvector)`
   - **Your role**: `Lead Full-Stack Mobile & Backend Architect (Flutter, .NET 8, PostgreSQL, AI RAG)`
   - **Project description**: Copy the 584-character summary.
   - **Skills and deliverables**: Select the recommended tags (`Flutter`, `Dart`, `ASP.NET Core`, `.NET 8`, `PostgreSQL`, `pgvector`, `OpenSearch`, `AI RAG`, `Clean Architecture`, `Mobile App Development`, etc.).
3. Upload all 6 graphics from the [`images/`](./images/) directory, setting **`01_cover_thumbnail.png`** as your project cover image.
4. Click **Publish** to showcase your enterprise mobile & AI solution!

## 🔍 Technical Highlights at a Glance

- **Cross-Platform Mobile:** Flutter 3.22+, Riverpod, Drift (SQLite) with WASM, dynamic RTL/LTR Persian/English layout.
- **Enterprise Backend:** ASP.NET Core 8 Web API, MediatR CQRS, FluentValidation, SignalR streaming.
- **Data & Search Infrastructure:** PostgreSQL 16 with `pgvector`, OpenSearch 2.x BM25, Redis 7 caching, Docker Compose.
- **Intelligent RAG AI:** Hallucination-free QA grounded in authentic Tafsir Nemoneh, Al-Mizan, and Ibn Kathir with Gemini/Grok API adapters.
- **Audio Streaming & Telemetry:** Verse-by-verse continuous audio recitation with lockscreen notifications & Web Admin portal.
